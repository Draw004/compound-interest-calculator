HOW IT WORKS

Compounding frequency changes the effective annual rate.

The starting principal uses the nominal annual rate and selected compounding frequency. Optional contributions are modeled at an equivalent periodic rate derived from the effective annual rate so contribution cadence can remain independent of compounding cadence.

Principal growthFV = P × (1 + j/n)^(n×t)
Effective annual rateEAR = (1 + j/n)^n − 1
IMPORTANT CONTEXT

Use the result as a scenario, not a forecast.

A bank deposit can have a stated interest convention. Market investments do not earn a smooth guaranteed rate, so an entered investment return should be interpreted as an assumption used to understand compounding mechanics.
